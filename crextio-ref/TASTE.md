# Design taste - what "looks like us" means

Researched 2026-11-28 from the 36 Crextio case images in this folder, the user's own words across past sessions, and
the web references below. `NOTES.md` (same folder) lists what was already taken from Crextio; this file is the
"why" behind it, so any person or agent can judge a new screen without re-asking.

The one-line version: **a calm, warm instrument.** Cream paper, charcoal ink, sunlight-gold used only where the eye
must land, numbers set large and light, data drawn as simple glyphs (pills, dots, hatching) instead of chart junk,
motion that is quick and purposeful. Premium because it is restrained and consistent, not because it is decorated.

---

**Decided by the user (2026-11-28): Crextio, not Zelt, is the look.** Zelt and the other references below only
help where Crextio is silent (dense tables, long forms, real data volumes) - when they disagree, Crextio wins on
looks and the compact-first rule wins on spacing.

## 2. Crextio, taken apart

Crextio is a 2025 concept by **Nixtio** (concept and art direction Bogdan Nikitin): web dashboard, mobile app, brand,
social and motion, 12 weeks. Typeface **Outfit** (geometric sans, used at 300-500). Dribbble palette: gold `#E6A434` /
`#F5D175`, charcoal `#0A0807`, cream `#F0ECDD` / `#EBE7D7`, grey `#A8A49E`. Nixtio's sibling "HR Platform UI" adds a
warm terracotta `#9C443C` - a negative/alert tone that belongs to the same family, unlike a pure red.

### The material
- **Canvas is light, not flat:** cool grey at the top left warming to a soft gold glow at the bottom right. The
  warmth comes from light falling on the page, not from tinted cards.
- **Cards are paper on paper:** near-white, very large radius (~24-28 px), no borders, a shadow you barely see.
  Separation comes from tone, not lines.
- **One charcoal card per screen** (Onboarding Task, Attendance Report, Test Statistics) - the "dark chapter" that
  holds the most important live data. On phone, one bright **gold card** plays the same role (Birthday).
- **Gold is a highlight on data, not a button colour.** The active nav pill is *charcoal*, the main CTA is often a
  white pill. Gold marks: the current bar, the current hour, the "hired" share, a done tick, the line of "you".

### Type and numbers
- Headlines in **light weight, large**: "Hello Valentina", "Hiring", "Devices". Never bold display type.
- **Headline numerals** huge and light (78 / 56 / 203) with a tiny icon and a small label underneath - no box.
- Labels in **sentence case**, small, muted grey. No uppercase tracked kickers anywhere.

### The data glyphs (Crextio's real signature)
- **Segmented pill strip:** one row split into charcoal / gold / hatched / outlined pills, each with its % inside
  ("Interviews 70% · Hired 10% · Project time 15% · Output 5%"). The same idea scores a candidate (Experience,
  Skills, Interview, Testing).
- **Hatching (diagonal stripes)** means "remaining / other / not yet" - in bars, pills, empty calendar slots, even
  around the pricing card. **Outlined pill** means "small / zero".
- **Dot matrix:** a grid of dots, charcoal and gold, inside the dark card - attendance per day at a glance.
- **Vertical capsule bars** (week of hours): thin pills with a gold fill rising inside a hatched track.
- **Ring gauges** (time tracker 02:35, score 9,7) - thick gold arc on a pale track, number in the middle.
- **Line charts:** "you" in solid gold, "other" in grey dashes; the hover tooltip is a small dark pill with a dot.
- **Dotted timeline:** time chips (09:00, 10:00) as outlined pills joined by a dotted line, the current hour gold,
  events as cards beside it - the current event in charcoal.

### Controls
- **Pill navigation bar** inside a pale capsule, active item charcoal; settings / notifications / account as round
  icon buttons on the right.
- **Round ↗ button** at the top right of every card - "open this".
- Round icon buttons for call / message / play / pause; pagination as round number chips with ← → circles.
- Accordions with **dotted dividers** (Pension contributions, Devices, Compensation summary).

### People
- Photography is the emotional layer: big portrait at the top of a profile, fading into a warm brown glass panel
  that holds the facts. Avatar stacks on events. (We use real employee photos only when they exist; never stock.)

## 4. The taste, as rules

1. **Light, warm, paper.** Warm off-white canvas with a soft gold light; cream/white cards; charcoal ink, never pure
   black on pure white.
2. **One loud thing per screen.** At most one charcoal card and one gold highlight cluster. If two things are gold,
   one is wrong.
3. **Gold marks data, charcoal marks state.** Selected tab, active nav, primary toggle = charcoal (invert). Gold =
   "this is you / now / the number that matters" and the single main action.
4. **Numbers are the heroes.** Big, light, tabular, unboxed, with a small label. A stat tile is never bigger than its
   number deserves.
5. **Draw data with the glyph kit, not chart libraries' defaults:** segmented pill strip, hatching for remainder,
   outlined pill for zero, dot matrix for days, capsule bars, ring gauge, solid-gold-vs-grey-dashed lines.
6. **Sentence case, light headlines, one family.** No uppercase kickers, no bold display type, no second typeface
   except where the spec already allows it.
7. **Pills and circles for things you press; big soft radius for things that hold content.** Every card that leads
   somewhere gets the round ↗ button in the same corner.
8. **Structure felt, not seen.** Tone and spacing before borders; dotted or hairline dividers only where tone fails.
9. **Compact beats airy.** Take Crextio's material, not its spacing. More useful content on the first screen.
10. **Function wins.** A visual idea that hides data, adds a click or slows a daily task is dropped.
11. **Everywhere or nowhere.** A pattern adopted on one page is rolled out to every page that has the same thing.
12. **Real data only.** No invented metrics, no stock people, no decoration that pretends to be information.

## 5. Motion, the same way

- Animate only to show **where something came from, what changed, or that the system heard you**. Never because it
  "looks cool" on something seen 50 times a day.
- Frequency decides: keyboard and constant actions get no animation; hovers barely any; drawers, dialogs and toasts
  the standard; first-time moments (a run finalised, onboarding complete) may delight.
- `ease-out` for entering and leaving (strong curve, e.g. `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`), `ease-in-out` for moving
  on screen, never `ease-in`. UI under 300 ms; drawers and dialogs up to ~500 ms.
- Springs (bounce 0.1-0.2) for things you drag or that can be interrupted; transitions rather than keyframes for
  anything that can fire twice in a second.
- Crextio-specific moments worth motion: numbers counting up once on first load, pill strips and gauges filling from
  zero, dot-matrix dots appearing in a short wave, the ↗ button nudging on card hover.
- Only `transform` and `opacity`; `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps fades, drops movement.

## 6. The "does this look like us?" check

Before calling a screen done, look at the screenshot and answer:

1. Squint: is there exactly one dark block and one gold focus? Does the eye land on the most important number first?
2. Are the headline numbers large and light, with small sentence-case labels and no boxes?
3. Is every proportion / progress / mix shown with the glyph kit (pills, hatching, dots), not a default chart?
4. Is the selected/active state charcoal, and gold kept for data and the one main action?
5. Any uppercase kickers, bold headlines, hard borders or rainbow badges left? Remove them.
6. Does every card that opens something have the round ↗ in the top-right corner?
7. Is it at least as compact as before - is half of the first screen real content?
8. Would a Crextio screenshot and this screenshot side by side look like the same product family?
9. Is every animation answering "why does this move?", fast, and off for reduced motion?
10. Is the same pattern used on every other page that shows the same kind of thing?

## 7. Open questions for the user

- **Typeface.** Crextio uses Outfit; we use Geist (which Nothing OS also adopted). Outfit would make the
  resemblance much stronger in headlines and numerals; Geist reads better in dense tables. Option: Outfit for
  headlines and headline numbers only, Geist for everything else. Needs your decision (a new font package).
- **Negative tone.** Nixtio's own HR palette uses terracotta `#9C443C` instead of a bright red. Our negative text
  (`--negative-ink` `#a3392f`) is already close; the fill (`--destructive` `#d4574e`) is a brighter coral. Moving the
  fill toward terracotta would sit better on cream. Your call.
- **Not yet built from Crextio** (from `NOTES.md`): the segmented pill strip as an attendance-mix widget, and the
  dot-matrix attendance chart for managers / HR.
